Privacy Policy

Dart Bandawasa

Last Updated: August 17, 2026

Dart Bandawasa is operated by Karya Putra Tabanan ("we", "us", or "our").

This Privacy Policy explains how Dart Bandawasa ("the App") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our services.

By using Dart Bandawasa, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Our Services

Dart Bandawasa is a transportation and delivery platform that allows users to register and use the application as:

  • A passenger or customer requesting motorcycle or car transportation.
  • A customer requesting delivery or courier services.
  • A driver providing transportation or delivery services.
  • A merchant or business partner providing products or services through the platform.

A registered account may use one or more of these roles depending on the services available to the user.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account and Personal Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Full name or name provided by the user.
  • Email address.
  • Phone number.
  • Account and authentication-related information.
  • User role, such as customer, driver, or merchant.

B. Driver and Vehicle Information

If you register or operate as a driver, we may collect information related to your vehicle, including:

  • Vehicle type.
  • Vehicle name or model.
  • Vehicle license plate number.
  • Other information necessary to verify and operate the driver service.

C. Location Information

Dart Bandawasa uses location information to provide transportation and delivery services.

Depending on the service being used, we may collect and process:

  • Your current or pickup location.
  • Your destination location.
  • Driver location.
  • Delivery-related locations.
  • Location information associated with an active trip or delivery.

Location information is used to:

  • Match customers with nearby drivers.
  • Display pickup and destination locations.
  • Allow customers to see the driver's location during an active service.
  • Allow drivers to navigate to customers and destinations.
  • Calculate routes, distance, and estimated travel information.
  • Support transportation and delivery operations.
  • Improve the reliability and safety of our services.

Location access is requested when it is necessary for the relevant functionality of the App. Users may control location permissions through their device settings.

3. Google Maps Platform

Dart Bandawasa uses Google Maps Platform and related Google Maps services to provide map, location, routing, and navigation-related functionality.

Google Maps services may process information such as location coordinates and other information required to provide mapping functionality.

Your use of Google Maps functionality is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

For more information, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

4. Payment and Top Up Information

Dart Bandawasa provides functionality for users to add balance or perform Top Up transactions.

We may process information related to your balance, transaction history, Top Up status, and other information necessary to maintain your account balance and transaction records.

We do not intend to collect or store sensitive payment credentials such as your full bank card number unless explicitly required by an integrated payment provider.

Where third-party payment services are used, payment information may be processed by the relevant payment service provider in accordance with its own privacy policy and terms.

5. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  1. Create and manage user accounts.
  2. Provide transportation and delivery services.
  3. Connect customers with drivers.
  4. Connect customers with merchants or business partners.
  5. Verify driver and vehicle information.
  6. Display relevant driver, vehicle, pickup, and destination information.
  7. Provide maps, routes, and navigation functionality.
  8. Process and maintain Top Up and transaction records.
  9. Send service-related notifications.
  10. Communicate with users regarding their accounts or services.
  11. Detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity.
  12. Maintain the security and reliability of the App.
  13. Troubleshoot technical problems.
  14. Comply with applicable laws and legal requirements.
  15. Improve and maintain our services.

6. Notifications

Dart Bandawasa may send notifications to your device.

Notifications may include:

  • Ride or delivery status updates.
  • Driver assignment information.
  • Order or delivery updates.
  • Account-related information.
  • Top Up or transaction notifications.
  • Important service announcements.

You can manage notification permissions through your device settings where supported.

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information when necessary to provide the services requested by you.

Customers and Drivers

When a customer requests a ride or delivery, relevant information may be shared between the customer and assigned driver, such as:

  • Name.
  • Pickup location.
  • Destination.
  • Relevant service information.
  • Driver and vehicle identification information.

This sharing is necessary to facilitate the requested transportation or delivery service.

Merchants

When you use a merchant-related service, relevant order or service information may be shared with the applicable merchant to fulfill your request.

Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to support our App and services, including:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers.
  • Mapping and location service providers.
  • Notification services.
  • Payment or Top Up service providers.
  • Technical service providers.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or valid request from an authorized government authority.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account and transaction records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our agreements.

When information is no longer required for these purposes, we may delete or anonymize it in accordance with applicable requirements and our operational needs.

10. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their Dart Bandawasa account and associated personal information.

Account deletion requests may be submitted through the available account deletion functionality in the App or by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for legitimate business purposes, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or financial and transaction recordkeeping.

11. Children's Privacy

Dart Bandawasa is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use transportation, delivery, or related services under applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services to provide certain functionality.

These services may have their own privacy policies governing how information is processed.

Third-party services used by the App may include Google Maps Platform and other infrastructure, notification, authentication, payment, or service providers necessary for Dart Bandawasa to operate.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the beginning of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we handle personal information.
